Framing adds a Guides Only mode for composition without changing the camera's native aspect ratio, JPEG dimensions, or RAW crop settings. The composition frame appears in native 3:2. Crop remains the default, and the Mask setting stays independent of the mode.

Composition guides also follow native aspect ratios, including 1:1 and the GR IV 4:3 and 16:9 ratios.
