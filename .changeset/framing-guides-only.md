Framing adds a Guides Only mode for composition. Selecting a ratio sets the camera's native aspect to 3:2 in both modes. After that change, the composition frame does not change JPEG dimensions or RAW crop settings. Crop remains the default, and the Mask setting stays independent of the mode.

Composition guides also follow native aspect ratios, including 1:1 and the GR IV 4:3 and 16:9 ratios.
