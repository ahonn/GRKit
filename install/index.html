Install

  1. Turn the camera off.
  2. Open GRKit.zip (some browsers unzip it for you). Copy the contents of SD_ROOT, not the folder itself, to the card root. The card root then has the SCRIPT folder.
  3. Insert the card. First time only: hold MENU while you turn the camera on, then wait until it turns itself off. This step uses 00078350.588 and DEVELOP.MOD.
  4. Turn the camera on. GRKit loads about 8 to 10 seconds after power-on. Then press ADJ while you shoot and select the sixth entry, GRK.

    To load GRKit sooner, try Fast start.

  5. Optional: delete 00078350.588 and DEVELOP.MOD from the card root. Updates do not need them.

Update

  1. Turn the camera off.
  2. Delete the SCRIPT folder from the card.
  3. Open the new GRKit.zip. From SD_ROOT, copy only the SCRIPT folder to the card root.
  4. Insert the card and turn the camera on. You do not need to hold MENU.
  5. Film looks already on the card stay. To hide one, turn it off in Options of Film Look in the GRKit section of MENU.

Uninstall

  1. Turn the camera off. Delete the SCRIPT and GRKIT folders from the card. GRKIT holds your GRKit settings.
  2. To turn SD scripts off too, download the files below. Copy the contents of SD_ROOT to the card root.
  3. Insert the card. Hold MENU while you turn the camera on, then wait until it turns itself off.
  4. Remove 00078350.588 and DEVELOP.MOD from the card root.

FAQ

GRK does not show up in ADJ.

GRKit loads about 8 to 10 seconds after power-on. It takes longer on GR IV and on the first start after an install or update. Wait, then press ADJ while you shoot and select the sixth entry, GRK. The first time, SD scripts must be on: hold MENU while you turn the camera on. To load it sooner, try Fast start.

Why do my first photos after power-on have no film look?

Until GRKit has loaded, the camera uses its own settings. Wait a few seconds after power-on, or try Fast start.

Does GRKit change the camera firmware?

No. GRKit loads from the SD card while the camera is on. Without the card, or after you uninstall GRKit, the camera works as before. The SD script setting, which lets the camera run GRKit from the card, stays on until you turn it off. Only the optional fast-boot firmware changes the firmware, and only if you install it.

Can GRKit load faster?

Yes, with the optional fast-boot firmware. It needs a firmware update.

Does a film look change my Image Control settings?

No. While a film look is on, it replaces Image Control. Your Image Control settings come back when you turn the look off.

What happens when I format the card?

A format deletes GRKit and its settings. Before a format, copy the GRKIT folder to a computer. After the format, install GRKit again, then copy the GRKIT folder back to the card root.

Do I hold MENU at power-on again after an update or with another card?

No. You hold MENU only the first time, to turn on SD scripts. The setting stays on for every card.

Can I use my own LUT or Lightroom preset?

Yes. Select Import a LUT in Film Looks. For a Lightroom preset, the import window tells you how to turn it into a LUT image.

Can I use these looks in Lightroom?

Not directly: GRKit does not make Lightroom presets. The looks come from open-source film LUTs. Film look licenses, at the bottom of the page, gives the source of each look.

Which cameras does GRKit support?

GR III, GR IIIx, GR IV, their HDF models, and GR IV Monochrome without film looks, on the firmware versions in the camera list. Earlier GR models are not supported.

Which GRKit version is on my card?

In the camera, open MENU. About GRKit is at the end of the GRKit section in the Customize tab.
