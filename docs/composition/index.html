Framing

Where
ADJ › GRK › Framing
JPEG
Cropped to the ratio
DNG
Stays 3:2

Press Fn to set Mode and Mask. Guides Only shows the frame and keeps the whole photo.

Crop

Where
ADJ › GRK › Crop
Choices
35, 40, 50, 70 mm
GR IIIx
50, 57, 71, 100 mm

Press Fn to set Mode and Mask. Frame Only shows the frame and keeps the whole photo. To keep the whole sensor picture in the DNG, turn on Uncropped RAW.

Guides

Thirds, Center, Golden Ratio, Diagonals, Golden Spiral, and Dynamic Symmetry. The lines are on the screen only, not in the photo. Press Fn to set Color, Opacity, and Hide with DISP.
