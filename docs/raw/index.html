RAW development

GRKit does not change the raw data. Develop a DNG in the camera with GRKit settings.

  1. Open a DNG in the playback mode and start RAW development.
  2. To change the film look, open the Image Control list.
  3. To set Grain, Glow, Border, or Date Imprint, open the GRK row at the end of the list.
  4. Run the development.

Development starts from the GRKit settings that the DNG records. The GRK values apply to that development only, and the Framing ratio stays.

On a computer

XMP readers such as exiftool show the GRKit settings of a photo. With Uncropped RAW, Lightroom and Camera Raw open the DNG with the crop of the shot, and you can undo that crop.
