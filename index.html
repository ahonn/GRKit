On the camera

Before you install

Is GRKit free?

Yes. GRKit is free and open source on GitHub, under the MIT License. It needs no account.

Is it safe for my camera?

GRKit loads from the SD card and does not change the firmware, unless you choose to install the optional fast-boot firmware. Take out the card, and the camera works as before. GRKit comes with no warranty.

Which cameras does it work with?

GR III, GR IIIx, GR IV, their HDF models, and GR IV Monochrome without film looks. The install page lists the supported firmware versions. Earlier GR models are not supported.

What do I need?

An SD card and a card reader. On a computer, Chrome or Edge writes GRKit straight to the card. In other browsers, download GRKit.zip and copy it to the card.

Does GRKit change my RAW files?

No. GRKit changes the JPEG. The RAW (DNG) file keeps the raw data from the camera.

Does it slow down the camera?

GRKit loads about 8 to 10 seconds after power-on. Until then, the camera uses its own settings. With the optional fast-boot firmware, it loads in one to two seconds.

How do I remove it?

Uninstall it on the install page, or delete the GRKIT and SCRIPT folders from the card. Your photos stay.

Safe to try
